Welcome

Welcome to Willow Creek Community Garden, a friendly place where neighbours come together to grow fresh food, share knowledge, and enjoy the outdoors. Whether you're planting your first seeds & soil or you're an experienced gardener, there's a place for you here.

New members are always welcome! Bring your curiosity, a pair of gardening gloves, and a willingness to learn.

What you can grow

  • Tomatos
  • Carrots
  • Lettuce
  • Herbs
    • Basil
    • Parsley
Growing food here has helped me make friends and enjoy fresh vegetables right outside my door. - Jamie, garden member

Want to learn more about growing your own food? Visit the Royal Horticultural Society for helpful gardening advice

How to Rent a Plot

  1. Complete a membership application form.
  2. Choose an available garden plot.
  3. Pay the yearly plot fee.
  4. Attend the new-member orientation.

Plot Fees

plot size dimensions yearly fee
Small 1 m × 2 m $25
Medium 2 m × 3 m $45
Large 3 m × 4 m $65
